## LinkedIn draft

I published an open AI & ML Engineering Portfolio guide for people deciding what to build and how to show their work.

It lays out two complementary project briefs:

• Demand Replay — next-day forecasting with clear data boundaries, simple baselines and temporal evaluation.
• Evidence Desk — a documentation assistant with citations, abstention and an evaluation plan.

It also includes a beginner CSV project, a reliability release plan, an open-source contribution path, and editable README, evaluation, case-study and application templates.

The research reviewed 20 job postings from 11 employers and 25 source documents, dated 7 November 2026. That is a targeted sample, not a global hiring survey. This is AI-assisted research and project guidance; the proposed ML systems are not completed implementations or claimed performance results.

The guide is free to read, portable, and MIT-licensed for its original material. You can choose a role focus, adjust the study budget and export a personal plan in the browser.

## A useful reply when someone asks what to build

If it directly answers their question and the community permits self-promotion:

> I put together a guide on [specific problem they asked about]. The relevant section explains [one concrete benefit]. The project is still a proposal, with [relevant limit], and the evaluation/template is here: [direct section link]. I maintain the guide, so feedback is welcome.

Replace every bracketed field. Do not paste this into unrelated conversations. Disclose your connection to the project. A helpful answer can stand on its own before the link.

## Before posting

- Check the guide, image and download links on the device you will use.
- Describe the guide as research and project specifications; do not imply the publisher has trained or deployed the proposed ML systems.
- Keep source dates and sample limits attached to hiring claims.
- Remove unimplemented feature claims and placeholder fields.
- Respect each community's self-promotion and posting rules.
- Prefer a specific request for feedback over a request for engagement.
